Controller laboratory

Engineering shapes — not final art. Local 60 Hz controller fixture, no multiplayer connection. Cyan: collider; amber: root; green: support; magenta: contact normal; orange: grounded patrol enemy; blue: clearance-checked walkable root spans (static geometry only).

Focus the surface, then arrows or WASD to move/aim/crouch; Space to jump; Enter to step while paused; Down + Space to drop. A tap is retained until the next tick. Blur pauses and clears input. Jump-link/drop-link and route-chain scenarios use the authored-route button to start automatic traversal. Purple shows the validated path; removal cancels it. In enemy-route, the orange enemy plans independently while you control cyan. At most 3,600 ticks per recording.

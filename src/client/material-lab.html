Weapon material laboratory

Compare a two-pixel barrier with stationary or patrolling calibration targets. Targets have 32 HP to expose repeated hits. One keyboard drives the selected party. These boxes show engineering collision geometry, attack volumes and impact points.

Focus the surface. Arrows move and aim; Z fires or selects a nearby knife action; C throws a grenade; Space jumps. Enter advances one tick while paused. Hold Down for the calibrated grenade throw. Release Z before stepping to test a short tap. A grenade starts farther back so its real bounce and fuse can be compared with the blast radius.

Cyan: player bodies. Pink: target bodies. Gray, tan, blue or green: cover. Yellow: projectiles and shot volumes. Orange: flame. Pale cyan: laser. Red: recent impact points.

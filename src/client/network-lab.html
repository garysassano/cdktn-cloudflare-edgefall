Network controller laboratory

Engineering graphics. Four local browser clients share an authoritative room. Cyan is the predicted local collider; orange is the received local collider; other players use their received state. This fixture does not claim final art, combat, remote interpolation or live scheduling acceptance.

Focus the game and use arrows/WASD, Space to jump and Down to crouch. The client captures at 60 Hz independently of snapshots and normally batches at 20 Hz. Z/X/E/V exercise fire, grenade, interact and vehicle-special input acknowledgments; this movement fixture has no combat effects. Input is neutralized on blur. A mapping fault stops prediction and requires a fresh server baseline.

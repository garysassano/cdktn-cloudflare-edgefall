# Diagnostic combat archive format 16

The combat laboratory uses archive **16**, simulation/recording format **13**, protocol **3.13** and combat snapshot section **4**. Breakwater mission/recording format is **4**. These versions reject earlier experimental continuation because material response and clipped solid-face contact change simulation behavior. The public snapshot layout has not changed.

This extends [format 15](combat-checkpoint-v15.md). The diagnostic content digest now includes the complete surface-material registry and each destructible's material binding. Existing scaffold and Breakwater props explicitly use timber. Material definitions are immutable content inputs, while private/public prop state still retains identity, health and destruction attribution. No new material field is inferred from an older checkpoint.

Concrete blocks every attack family and takes no damage. Timber blocks every family and preserves the prior unit damage scale. Armor steel blocks every family, ignores bullets, heat, blades and blunt hits, takes ordinary explosive damage and double energy damage. Open grating blocks bullets, blades and blunt damage propagation; blast, heat and energy may pass through it. It takes ordinary bullet, explosive, energy and blunt damage, and ignores heat and blades. Actor movement and released rocket/grenade bodies still collide with physical grating.

The same material policy governs muzzle clearance, swept bullets, rectangle occlusion, impact-time blast occlusion, flame propagation and instantaneous beams. A volume clipped to a damageable solid face still records contact, while a single corner or ordinary body graze does not. A contacted flame lobe retains its propagation limit after a solid moves or is destroyed; later emitted lobes may advance through the cleared space. Incoming impact damage remains raw attack data; prop health applies the registered material scale during damage commitment.

The separate `material-lab.html` inspector uses local recording format **1**. Its recording contains an exact definition, retained input commands, a local content fingerprint and canonical final state. Import reconstructs from registered content, validates each command, replays the recording, and compares final state before replacing the inspector world. Its fingerprint is a compatibility check, not authentication. These calibration stages and 32-HP dummies are not registered diagnostic room scenarios or accepted durable checkpoints. Their material-specific room, snapshot and cold-process integration remains separate work.

See the [material coverage evidence](redesign-evidence/W07-materials.md) for portable runtime, browser geometry and existing diagnostic storage/mission regression results. Production v3 integration, deployed cadence and live trace ingestion remain unverified.

# W03 bounded kinematic movement

Status: `src/game/physics/move.ts` adds pure movement resolution and persistent support on top of the exact sweep queries. It remains an engineering kernel; the active v2 game has not been switched to it. The complete controller, collision laboratory, shared prediction, compiler and W02 acceptance gates remain required.

## Movement and failure contract

`moveKinematic` accepts a positive-area world-space body rectangle, its own integer tick motion, its previous support ID and immutable terrain with authored tick displacements. It returns a new pose, clipped own motion, support identity, ordered contact diagnostics and an explicit status. It never mutates its inputs. The controller must convert root-relative shapes to this world rectangle; texture dimensions and animation callbacks have no role in this API.

Each sweep chooses the full earliest contact manifold, advances the body and terrain toward the rational contact with integer rounding toward their starts, and requantizes every remaining displacement. Tangential displacement is retained, so sliding does not lose the fraction discarded at the intermediate contact. Only the body's own motion into blocking normals is cancelled. Moving obstacles can push the body, while support carry is added once and kept separate from own velocity. Own motion plus carry must fit the existing ±2^16-subpixel tick bound; invalid authored speeds throw instead of silently saturating. The result's contact time is relative to its numbered residual sweep, not a cumulative whole-tick timestamp or a serialized controller contact field.

Resolution is limited to four contact rounds. Initial overlap is eligible for a minimal cardinal correction of at most one logical pixel, considering at most eight overlapping solids and 32 candidate translations. Corrections cannot cross a previously separate thin obstacle or leave the coordinate bounds. Larger/unresolvable overlaps return `initial-overlap`; they do not trigger a larger push or spawn teleport. Level compilation must still reject invalid spawns. Normal movement, tiny correction and failure recovery have distinct semantics.

Only `complete` is an accepted end-of-tick pose. `contact-limit`, `unresolved-contact` and `residual-overlap` require the owning world step to stop/recover; `crushed` requires an explicit gameplay lifecycle event before advancing surviving bodies. Failure poses are partial diagnostics, never an excuse to advance geometry around a supposedly valid living body. Runtime integration of those decisions is still pending.

## Support, moving surfaces and crush

Exact top-plane contact can establish support. At overlapping seams the previous valid support wins; otherwise stable entity ID chooses. One subpixel of separation is tolerated only for a previously supported or actually impacted surface, accounting for conservative relative-motion rounding. Nearby unrelated terrain cannot manufacture support. Walking past the supporting extent or removing its ID clears support; the next gravity step falls normally. This kernel does not itself increment geometry revision or journal platform destruction; the world owner must do that at the tick boundary.

Riders follow sideways/upward/downward platform displacement once. Upward own motion releases carry for jumping. A rising platform can catch an initially unsupported body. One-way terrain accepts only the existing relative downward crossing rule, and ignoring one selected support leaves lower platforms active. Sliding along a wall while a floor moves sideways is not a crush.

Crush requires incompatible opposing surfaces. Exact touching faces with closing relative motion prove immediate crush. When conservative rounding leaves a one-subpixel gap on either side, the solver checks the rational closure time, requires perpendicular overlap throughout that interval and rules out an earlier side contact that could change the trajectory. Uncertain corner/escape cases return a diagnostic instead of turning numerical uncertainty into a death. A reduced two-obstacle reproduction from generated seed 66 covers diagonal closure, and an early sideways escape fixture prevents treating every future narrowing corridor as a crush.

The inherited sweep bounds still apply. Cardinal correction differences stay within 2^25; each tested correction is at most 256 subpixels. Target/body residual components remain within ±2^16. Opposing relative motion is at most 2^17, witnessed gap totals at most two subpixels, and closure/side-contact comparison products stay within 2^42, with summed expressions within 2^43. No rational denominators are multiplied across contact rounds, and no nonfinite value is stored in state.

## Validation and limits

`pnpm check` passes 166 tests across 17 files, lint, asset/content validation, all TypeScript targets, client/Worker builds and CDKTN synthesis. Twenty-one new tests cover floor/wall/ceiling/corner resolution, tangential remainder preservation, contact limits, input immutability, seam support, ledges, removed support, one-way drops, carry, rising/sideways obstacles, crush, bounded correction and checkpoint restoration. One hundred seeded 120-tick gravity/jump routes complete without solid penetration and are identical under reversed terrain insertion order.

The 512 seeded moving-obstacle cases produce 499 completed, nonpenetrating poses and 13 proven crushes. None returns an unresolved status in this corpus; the tests explicitly reject those statuses rather than skipping every failed solve. This corpus is a deterministic invariant test, not a proof for all possible moving geometry or a representative combat CPU profile. The earlier 256-seed BigInt reference remains the exact narrow-phase oracle.

The [runtime report](./W03-movement-runtime-proof.json) records identical Node 24.20, Chromium 151 and local workerd results for all 512 moving cases, the existing 256 sweep cases and protocol contracts. A 1,200-tick engineering gravity/platform fixture has trace hash `8e99e568`, including a jump, reattachment, selected-platform drop, landing on the lower floor and a geometry revision change. Restoring only serialized kinematics after ticks 239, 601 and 899 reproduces that hash without retaining diagnostic contact history. The report's commit is the pre-change base; the source and bundle digests identify tested code. This is not complete controller/checkpoint recovery, a 20-minute gameplay replay, deployed cadence or Firefox/WebKit acceptance.

The subsequent [spatial indexing milestone](./W03-spatial-index.md) adds immutable static/per-tick grids with indexed/full-scan equivalence and a bounded local comparison.

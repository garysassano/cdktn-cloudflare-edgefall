# W05 representative shotgun and flame weapons

The deterministic combat lab and diagnostic room now include distinct shotgun and flame behaviors with exact visible damage rectangles. This supplies the representative weapon fixtures needed by the W06 benchmark. W05 still needs the lab tank and remaining grenade platform policies; full W07 weapon coverage, final media, authored missions and production v3 remain open.

## Behavior

Shotgun spends one of 24 shells for a six-tick blast expanding to 110 pixels, with a 28-tick firing cadence and twelve-tick recoil action. Each entity receives at most one four-damage body hit per trigger, even with overlapping hurtboxes. It uses a volume rather than pellet projectiles. The crouched blast trims its height against the floor while keeping its forward reach; frontal walls stop it.

Flame spends one of thirty charges for three lobes emitted at action offsets 0, 6 and 12. The eighteen-tick emission phase has twelve recovery ticks before the next charge. Each lobe follows the muzzle and aim for six ticks, then travels with a frozen root/heading for the remainder of its eighteen-tick life, up to ninety pixels. All lobes share one victim ledger and a six-tick damage cooldown. Earliest contact across lobes wins; overlapping lobes cannot multiply a tick's damage. A contacted wall caps propagation, including after removal. Side obstructions conservatively trim the rectangle's cross-section. Death cancels future emissions and attached lobes; detached flame and released shotgun blasts survive independently.

Heat removes one shield integrity per eligible hit. Breaking the two-integrity shield does not spill into body HP; a subsequent exposed body hit kills ordinary infantry. Volume damage uses moving hurtboxes, terrain and shield occlusion. Birth ticks and heading changes use endpoint exposures. A detached lobe cannot heat a shield outside its rectangle merely because the shield blocks a ray from the original muzzle.

Protocol **3.6** appends 48-byte rectangles in combat section **2**. Each carries source/action identity, owner, lobe, lifetime, heading and attachment. Clients draw these committed rectangles directly. Archive **7** retains the private emission cursor, clipped reach, cancellation and per-target cooldowns. Duplicate area groups for one action, altered active action origins and inconsistent ownership are rejected. Combat simulation and exported recordings use format **4**. The maximum snapshot allocation is **50,984 bytes**, including all 64 bounded area records; this is a hard format limit, not a traffic target. See the [snapshot](../snapshot-v3.md), [event](../events-v3.md) and [archive](../combat-checkpoint-v7.md) contracts.

## Review findings and limits

The early [probe](W05-area-first-probe.log.gz) exposed a crouched shotgun losing its forward reach when its expanding height touched the floor. Clipping now distinguishes cross-section obstruction from centerline blocking and is independent of terrain iteration order. Review also corrected birth-tick terrain timing, earliest-contact arbitration between lobes, out-of-volume shield hits, duplicate restored damage ledgers and the stale total-frame allocation ceiling. Focused regressions cover these boundaries, all four headings, a moving victim crossing an exposure, retained thin-wall blocking and cancelled/blocked emissions.

The first [focused test run](W05-area-first-focused.log.gz) expected a reduced contact fraction and treated the newly valid section version as corruption. The [second](W05-area-second-focused.log.gz) compared JavaScript negative zero directly with wire zero. Assertions now compare the exact rational value and canonical snapshot while still comparing the complete area rectangles directly. These were verifier mismatches, not reasons to weaken collision or recovery policy.

Final [shotgun diagnostics](W05-area-shotgun-diagnostics.json.gz) and [flame diagnostics](W05-area-flame-diagnostics.json.gz) retain favicon 404s and aborted local admission/session/profile requests. Neither run reports a page exception, protocol error, persistence failure or clock fault. Gameplay cadence, catch-up, input lead, event retention and storage backlog guards are unchanged. The existing host-clock/input-lead observations remain unresolved for sustained deployed operation.

Wrangler configuration still explicitly enables traces and invocation logs at sampling 1 and uploads source maps. This increment performs no Cloudflare deployment or live trace retrieval. Whole-suite conformance durations, recorded in the runtime report, are not per-tick CPU profiles; [observability notes](observability.md) retain the live-ingestion limitation.

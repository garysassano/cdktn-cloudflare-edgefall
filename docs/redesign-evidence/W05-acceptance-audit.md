# W05 grounded combat and tank acceptance audit

This audit checks the current implementation against the grounded combat and vehicle laboratory requirements. The lab has enough implemented behavior to exercise most of the representative art benchmark, but W05 is not complete. The remaining HMG sweep and integrated combat/terrain-boundary proof are concrete implementation work. Human style acceptance, final weapon/vehicle coverage and production networking remain separate open gates.

W04 impairment/input-lead/clock findings, production v3, W07/W08 completion, authored Harbor/Foundry/Carrier, results/outbox/rematch and staging/human gates remain open.

# W03 protected entry physics

Protected entry now consumes the same moving geometry and swept collision as living and dead bodies. Previously a rising lift could pass through a stationary player during the twelve-tick animation and cause an initial-overlap failure when control returned. The initial anchor now survives the full platform sweep before admission, and protected bodies keep moving until the living controller takes over.

Unsafe authored candidates are skipped in priority order. If an admitted entry is crushed or falls into the void, its body becomes absent and input remains unavailable while safe anchors are retried. This never spends another life. A successful retry restarts the entry animation and protection. Fresh jump/fire works on the first controllable tick with one movement step and one shot. Solver-confirmed crush while alive bypasses bullet protection, spends exactly one life and removes the body immediately.

Protocol **3.11**, archive **12** and simulation/recording **9** replace nullable `deathBody` with `bodyPresence: "present" | "removed"`. Alive requires present, spectating requires removed, and death/entry may carry either. Non-alive players cannot hold seats or active actions. Player records remain 304 bytes; minimum and maximum complete snapshots remain 448 and 52,060 bytes. Previous experimental formats fail closed. See the [wire contract](../snapshot-v3.md) and [entry/archive contract](../combat-checkpoint-v12.md).

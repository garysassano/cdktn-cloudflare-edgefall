# W05 tank controller and ownership

The deterministic combat lab and diagnostic Durable Object now support a controllable tank with one driver, grounded movement, a short jump, independent turret aim, an unlimited primary gun, armor and safe release. Four real keyboard clients can board, fight both rifle enemies and exit. This delivers the W05 tank interaction foundation; W05 still needs grenade platform policies and its final acceptance audit. The W08 cannon, sacrifice action, full vehicle matrix and final media remain open.

## Behavior and authority

The hull uses the shared swept grounded solver, including support, ceilings, moving-platform carry and crush outcomes. It moves at three pixels per tick and jumps with a five-pixel impulse and quarter-pixel gravity. Its eight-way turret slews one heading every three ticks, independently of hull facing. The primary gun releases through authored sockets every five ticks while fire is held, keeps a three-tick action cursor and never spends ammunition. Hand-to-muzzle terrain clearance can cancel a blocked release. Friendly bodies and tanks do not block or damage teammates.

The depot supplies four tanks in stable slot-associated positions. An interaction chooses the nearest eligible tank, with entity ID breaking an equal-distance tie. Claims from the same authoritative tick resolve by player slot; earlier accepted ticks retain their reservation. A reversed-arrival regression prevents a neighbor’s earlier input from starving a player at the depot. Boarding takes twelve ticks, following the seat socket; exit takes eight ticks. Voluntary release sweeps the full player collider to the facing-side, opposite-side and permitted upper candidates, using end-of-tick terrain. Blocked exit retains control. Exit grants twelve protected ticks and a thirty-tick reboard cooldown.

Three armor pips are separate from foot health. A damaging impact spends one pip per thirty-tick protection window. Destruction settles both entities before ejection, does not spill the hit into the player, and leaves a stationary visual-only wreck. A blocked emergency release or a tank crossing the fall boundary causes ordinary player death exactly once. Disconnected drivers have fifteen neutral simulated ticks before release; socket replacement, empty pause and recovery settle seats before a new baseline because an empty room does not keep ticking. A shared continue creates fresh enemy and depot vehicle IDs and spends one credit in the existing transaction.

Player input generations change with seat modes. The world commits every player, vehicle, action, event and acknowledgment together; multiple ownership causes in one tick advance a player once. Delayed commands retain their consumed sequence/edge cursors and cannot operate the new control mode. Vehicle lease and driver input generations are separate fields. The browser retains physical held keys while discarding unsampled action edges, and neutralizes pending commands from older generations during reconciliation. Tank movement currently follows authoritative snapshots; complete vehicle prediction and remote interpolation remain open.

Protocol **3.7** uses 324-byte vehicle records and a **51,240-byte** maximum complete snapshot allocation. This bound includes maximum populations and all 64 area records; it is not a bandwidth target. Archive **8**, combat section **2**, campaign continuation format **2** and local recording format **5** retain the new controller, action and ownership state. See the [wire contract](../snapshot-v3.md) and [private continuation contract](../combat-checkpoint-v8.md).

# W08: tank terrain and stage boundaries

Tanks now use the same authored fall boundary as on-foot players throughout the combat step. The selected stage profile reaches movement, boarding/exit, disconnect settlement, incoming-damage ejection and sacrifice commitment. The boundary is checked as a bounded integer before stepping. Registered diagnostic rooms retain their existing boundary; no wire, archive or media format changes.

Previously, tanks always used the laboratory's Y=248 death threshold. A valid depot on Y=360 was destroyed before boarding, and a descending lift killed its driver on otherwise supported ground. A stage with an earlier Y=232 boundary could instead reach the on-foot death handler while the player was still seated, throwing an ownership error. The updated order settles the vehicle and occupant together at the stage's own boundary.

The full combat-step verifier covers fourteen cases with one and four players. Each accepted step checks that live hulls do not overlap solid terrain at its end transform and that both seat links and the seat position remain consistent. Four-player terrain cases use separate simultaneous lanes, except for the shared tank-crossing case; these are geometry fixtures, not completed mission layouts.

| Cases                                      | Verified behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lower depot, sacrifice and incoming damage | Board, drive, exit, sacrifice and receive three authored rifle projectiles on Y=360 ground without accidental player death. Accepted impacts perform the armor debits.                                                                      |
| Ledge landing and fall                     | Land on a lower supported surface; crossing an earlier kill boundary destroys the tank and consumes exactly one player life without an ownership exception.                                                                                 |
| Step and ceiling                           | A 32-pixel step stops an unjumped hull, can be crossed with the actual jump, and supports landing. A separate jump hits the ceiling and resolves without overlap.                                                                           |
| Exact and undersized passage               | The hull passes an opening exactly its own height. A one-pixel-short opening blocks it, while retreat and voluntary exit remain usable.                                                                                                     |
| Descending lift and crush                  | Moving support carries the tank below the old fixed boundary and permits a safe exit. A closing ceiling/lift trap destroys the hull, releases the driver and returns the player at a supported checkpoint after one lost life.              |
| Blocked exit and forced disconnect         | Moving barriers close around an occupied hull. Voluntary exit preserves control until the route reopens; forced disconnect clears ownership and resolves ordinary death when every ejection path is blocked, followed by checkpoint return. |
| Friendly crossing                          | Four tanks cross on shared ground while firing primary guns and spending one shell each, without blocking or damaging allied hulls.                                                                                                         |

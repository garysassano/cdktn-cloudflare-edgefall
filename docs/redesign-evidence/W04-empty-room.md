# W04 durable empty-room pause and expiry

The combat laboratory now stops at the last accepted tick when its final player disconnects, confirms the remaining journal entries and persists a paused checkpoint. A reserved player's ordinary connection request restores that exact boundary and issues fresh input/effect generations. One ready connected player can restart while the other places remain reserved. An abandoned room expires through a Durable Object alarm, and its persisted terminal state cannot be recovered into another run. Production authority remains v2; this extends the [automatic reconnect milestone](./W04-automatic-reconnect.md) within the full redesign.

## Implemented boundary

The journal writer can seal a final segment of one to fourteen ticks after its pending full segment completes. It captures the accepted state before awaiting storage, rejects further records and advances its durable cursor only after a matching confirmation. A failed seal remains recoverable from the previous durable head. SQLite reconstruction accepts that bounded short tail, including the six-row checkpoint/head/segment view possible immediately before checkpoint rotation. Pause and expiry replace the checkpoint/head in one SQL transaction and prune the old segments.

Disconnect stops the clock immediately. If lease or reader expiry removes the final peer during a scheduled callback, the room explicitly declines the uncommitted tick; the clock does not increment its cursor. Accepted combat, projectiles, ammunition, kill accounting and event history remain frozen during the pause. In-flight admission claims settle before the paused boundary is confirmed, and new admission receives a retryable response while that write is pending.

The expiry deadline is the latest reserved member's durable timestamp plus ninety seconds. Waking an already disconnected membership does not extend that deadline or consume another membership epoch. Backward wall-clock samples cannot reduce its last recorded timestamp. The alarm is armed before the final pause write, with its handler rechecking membership and room state; returning players cancel it. Faulted empty rooms also arm expiry. A persisted paused or expired checkpoint loads without another recovery transition. A valid reserved connection restores a paused room to loading, with a fresh run epoch and zero old input, before the connected-player readiness/start barrier permits combat again.

The expiry handler is idempotent across repeated delivery and process replacement. It checks the stored deadline, reschedules early delivery, waits for a pending lifecycle transition, persists expiry and removes the alarm. This follows Cloudflare's [single-alarm and at-least-once delivery contract](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/api/alarms/). The handler owns rescheduling when it wakes a cold object; initialization does not overwrite the alarm currently being delivered.

## Preserved failure and remaining work

One attempt reached the tab-takeover stage, then a runtime clock regression stopped the room at tick 268, from 1788750191473 to 1788750190552. Independent Node samples show wall-time drops of 915 ms and 1,613 ms while monotonic time advances. The clients retry admission while the authority remains in recovery, and an expiry alarm is armed. This attempt and the passing rerun use the same source; no scheduling or input guard was relaxed. The host-clock cause and deployed timing gate remain unresolved. [Failed room](./W04-empty-room-clock-failure.json.gz), [failed client state](./W04-empty-room-clock-clients.json.gz), [independent diagnostics](./W04-empty-room-clock-diagnostics.json.gz).

The exact-tick guarantee applies after a normal empty pause has been confirmed. A process loss before confirmation still uses the existing bounded committed-prefix recovery policy. Membership admission/disconnect and alarm scheduling remain separate lifecycle writes; atomic production membership/result outbox, uncertain commit outcomes and exhaustive storage-failure interleavings remain open. Expiry retains a compact terminal checkpoint and private reservation metadata; production retention cleanup and result/replay publication are not implemented by this change.

Loading-peer replacement, full lobby/intermission/completed-mode admission, high-entropy production room capabilities, membership-epoch host commands, protected hostile reentry and vehicle ownership remain required. The laboratory start endpoint is an explicit test control; it does not establish production host authorization. Predicted action/effect confirmation, real transport impairment/visibility/soaks, hostile combat, tank interaction and the full three-mission campaign remain in progress. W04/G2, W05 and W10 stay open, with the W06 human style benchmark ahead of remaining final asset production.

Wrangler retains explicit traces and invocation logs at sampling 1 plus source-map upload. This increment performs no deployment or live trace retrieval; ingestion and deployed latency/costs remain unverified.

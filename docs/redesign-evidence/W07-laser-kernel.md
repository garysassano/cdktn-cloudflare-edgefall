# W07 laser collision and charge lifetime

The [laser kernel](../../src/game/combat/beam.ts) now queries an instantaneous beam after movement and retains one already-paid charge for its visible lifetime. Its calculations use bounded integers, accepted simulation ticks and cardinal muzzle directions. The [prepared laser definitions](../../src/game/content/weapons/laser.ts) specify 512 logical pixels of reach, a four-pixel width, two damage, an eight-entity penetration budget, six ticks per energy charge and 120 energy per pickup. The weapon is not yet registered in room combat; accepted input, ammunition, event delivery and durable room continuation are the next integration step.

Each pulse evaluates bodies and terrain at the end of its tick. A body crossing the beam entirely between samples takes no damage; a body ending inside it can take damage. This is a sampled beam, so the query does not sweep a 512-pixel projectile displacement or depend on render frequency. Ordinary targets are processed by forward distance and stable collider identity. Multiple hurt components sharing an entity consume one damage grant and one penetration slot. Reaching the target cap clips the visible beam at the last permitted contact.

Terrain takes priority at coincident contacts, followed by shields, solid bodies and ordinary bodies. Solid and one-way terrain stop energy. Any intersecting shield or solid prop stops the whole beam width, including partial-width cover; that conservative policy is explicit. Exposed body geometry ahead of its shield can receive one body grant, but the shield still stops propagation without a second grant to the entity. Owners and ordinary allies are excluded; allied solid bodies remain physical material. Energy is a distinct attack material with a prepared shield response.

`emitBeam` returns the birth pulse and its continuation state. `stepBeam` follows the accepted muzzle and recalculates clipping while the charge remains held, always returning an empty damage list. Release or an invalid owner removes the anchor and cancels the charge immediately. A six-tick charge born at tick 1 is visible through tick 6 and expires before tick 7, when the firearm layer may accept the next charge. Repeated or skipped steps, impossible restored ages, invalid geometry, ambiguous collider IDs and inconsistent attack policies are rejected. This helper assumes its caller has already accepted and paid for a new action; it does not admit input or enforce ammunition itself.

Long display geometry is split into at most four contiguous rectangles, each within the existing 256-pixel shape limit. The configured 512-pixel beam uses two segments when unobstructed. The birth result includes the same clipped geometry that determined its contacts. Room integration must retain that geometry in acknowledged event delivery: a short tap can finish before the next snapshot, and live snapshots alone cannot guarantee its display. No afterglow, wire extension or archive migration is claimed by this kernel increment.

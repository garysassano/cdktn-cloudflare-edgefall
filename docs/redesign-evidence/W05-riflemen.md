## Implemented behavior

The grounded rifleman acquires a visible, vulnerable player by distance and stable player-ID tie breaking. It commits target, cardinal aim and facing while raising its weapon. Its authored timeline raises for 24 ticks, releases three bullets at offsets 24, 30 and 36, and recovers until offset 72. A high, aligned target selects the up lane. Terrain blocks acquisition and hand-to-muzzle release; the enemy continues using the grounded physics kernel without following a player's height.

The burst cannot track a crossing target or be turned by patrol collision decisions. Killing the owner cancels unreleased markers; released bullets remain independent. The existing tick order permits same-tick trades because releases precede old-projectile impacts. Crouching avoids the level shot. Vulnerable player hurtboxes participate in swept collision, while protected entry does not absorb bullets for allies. Ordered damage spends one life even if two shots hit during the same tick. Hostile player deaths never add enemy encounter kill credit.

Protocol 3.3 adds explicit player/enemy origin to 64-byte event records, with a 4,128-byte maximum batch. Enemy releases have no player shot-confirmation key. Archive format 4 retains committed facing/target/timeline/action and consumed marker cursors, checks action ownership across actors, projectiles and retained events, and rejects earlier archive formats. See the [event contract](../events-v3.md) and [checkpoint contract](../combat-checkpoint-v4.md).

# Authoritative player lives and campaign transition candidates

The combat laboratory now consumes lives through actual controller falls, waits through death, enters at a collision-validated checkpoint with protection, and spectates at zero lives. Snapshot, prediction and committed-journal reconstruction preserve the independent life clock. Campaign helpers stage wipe, continue, rally, safe-exit and victory decisions for later world integration. Production remains v2; host continue/rematch remains unavailable.

## Behavior and compatibility

Three lives include the current life. A first authoritative lethal hit or fall decrements the count once; subsequent damage to a dead player cannot spend another life. Classic foot health is one, accessible foot health is three, and their shared continue budgets are three and nine respectively. An unprotected accessible player loses health before losing a life. Falls remain lethal through entry protection.

The provisional death delay is 30 ticks and the entry delay is 12 ticks. These are Edgefall tuning choices, not measured reference-game values. Entry starts with 120 ticks of hit protection, standing geometry, zero motion, the unlimited sidearm and ten grenades. It loses the special weapon while preserving shot ordinals and the last allocated action identity. Authored checkpoint candidates must have an unobstructed footprint and current support; a blocked or unsupported entry waits without spending another life. Vehicle release, moving-platform entry behavior, hostile threat clearance and final entry animation remain separate work.

[Protocol 3.2](../protocol-v3.md) adds unsigned `lifeStartTick` immediately after the player life enum. It is independent of action timing and cannot exceed the snapshot tick. Player records are 292 bytes; the minimum snapshot is 436 bytes and the hard complete-frame limit is 47,912 bytes. Input, acknowledgment and event record sizes remain unchanged. Earlier protocol minors and format-1 diagnostic archives fail closed; no production storage migration is implied. Independent Python `struct` updates to the checked-in input/snapshot goldens insert the life word and update the version/length fields without using the TypeScript encoder.

The four-stream recovery fixture exposed a fatal-fall/shot failure: damage cancelled the current action before the event adapter retrieved its firearm confirmation. The kernel now captures marker payload, control epoch and shot ordinal when a marker fires. A later death can cancel an action while its already emitted shot retains the correct confirmation. Private checkpoint validation checks this captured metadata; the wire event record remains 60 bytes.

## Campaign boundary scope

The pure reducers wait for remaining respawns, allow connected zero-life participants to reach a wipe despite disconnected reservations, and leave a fully disconnected party for the room's pause policy. A continue candidate requires the current wipe/checkpoint, safe entries for all participating actors and an available shared credit; failed entry leaves the original state and counters untouched. Once a candidate is installed, another continue is unavailable in its playing phase.

A next-mission candidate grants a zero-life member one rally life keyed to that mission. An already spent grant cannot be minted again, while a later mission can grant its own life. Living members retain their lives and carried inventory. Mission completion requires a validated complete final-encounter ledger, at least one connected living member, and eligible connected players' full body footprints inside the authored exit. Empty requirements alone, pending objectives, an invalid ledger or a party still awaiting respawn do not finish the mission. A simultaneous full wipe takes precedence over completion.

These are candidates for the world's atomic reset. The caller must still reconstruct checkpoint entities with fresh IDs, commit campaign/membership/input epochs and results together, publish replay boundaries and fence host commands. The three numbered reducer transitions are synthetic contract fixtures; they do not implement Harbor, Foundry or Carrier or prove authored mission traversal.

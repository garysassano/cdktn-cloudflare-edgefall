# Diagnostic combat archive format 11

Historical format 11 contract. Current body presence and protected entry use [format 12](combat-checkpoint-v12.md); format 11 archives are rejected.

The combat laboratory uses archive **11**, protocol **3.10**, combat snapshot section **3**, and simulation/recording format **8**. Earlier experimental schemas are rejected. This extends [format 10](combat-checkpoint-v10.md) with authoritative corpse presence and passive death physics; the deployed v2 product remains separate.

Every controlled player carries `deathBody: "present" | "removed" | null`. Only the death phase allows present or removed; alive, respawning and spectating require null. Dead players cannot own a seat or active action. Removed bodies retain their bounded last position, zero velocity and remainders, no support/contacts, false grounding and airborne locomotion. Snapshot and checkpoint validation reject inconsistent combinations. The wire stores a four-byte enum immediately after `lifeStartTick`, making each player record 304 bytes, the minimum snapshot 448 bytes and the maximum complete allocation 52,060 bytes.

A lethal hit cancels actions and debits one life while preserving accepted airborne velocity. Each following death tick applies the authored gravity and terminal speed through the same swept solver and support carry used by living bodies. The accepted standing or crouching footprint is retained. Ground contact stops horizontal motion; losing a support resumes falling. Entering the void removes the corpse immediately. Crush or failed bounded overlap/contact recovery retires it at the last valid position instead of committing an unresolved collision or stalling the room. Invalid content, geometry frames and unsupported remainders still fail validation.

Presence and position never reset `lifeStartTick` or debit another life. At the original thirty-tick deadline a player with lives tries the authored entry anchors; a blocked anchor waits while a present corpse continues physics or a removed corpse remains inert. A safe entry clears `deathBody` and starts the existing twelve-tick entry phase. Zero lives transitions to spectating on the original deadline. This change does not alter the protected entry phase's existing movement policy or add accessible-rule hurt behavior.

Private checkpoints, applied-input journals, movement prediction and public baselines preserve the same body and life clocks. Native presentation uses the authoritative body coordinates, holds an early death drawing while airborne, selects grounded death exposures on support and hides removed bodies. It never authorizes movement, damage or entry. The existing drawings remain a candidate; dedicated airborne acting and landing craft still require W06 review.

The [death-body proof](redesign-evidence/W03-death-body.md) covers passive collision cases, four admitted controllers killed by real rifle bullets, portable checkpoint/journal continuation, cold SQLite recovery and the keyboard-driven native inspector. This is local engineering evidence; production v3, deployed trace ingestion, sustained timing, authored missions and the complete human-reviewed style benchmark remain unfinished.

# Diagnostic combat archive format 9

The combat laboratory now uses archive **9**, protocol **3.8**, combat snapshot section **2**, and local simulation/recording format **6**. Earlier experimental archives and protocol minors are rejected. Production remains v2; this change does not migrate a deployed room. The [format 8 contract](combat-checkpoint-v8.md) describes the retained tank, ordnance and seat-ownership rules.

Each controlled player now retains `firearmAim: { pitch, nextStepTick }`. Pitch is the current authored barrel elevation from -4 (down) through 0 (horizontal) to 4 (up), mirrored horizontally by facing. The next step tick enforces the weapon's independent heading exposure. Neither field is reconstructed from requested aim, shot ordinal, action age, or a rendered frame. The HMG advances one heading every two ticks; retargeting starts from the accepted heading and preserves the outstanding exposure. Crouch lowers it immediately, while death and seating clear it. Unlimited-sidearm fallback settles to its cardinal direction.

The content digest includes heading vectors, muzzle sockets, timeline IDs and the exposure duration. The validator checks the nine authored headings, matching sockets and identical marker schedules; checkpoints also check aim bounds, future turn ticks, lowered-state neutrality and active firing pose. HMG projectile velocities must match an exact authored vector or its horizontal mirror. These rules preserve a single ammo debit and consumed marker cursor while changing direction.

The two fields add eight bytes to each public player record: 300 bytes per player, with at most thirty-two additional bytes per four-player snapshot. Binary input and acknowledgment records, 64-byte events, 324-byte vehicles and combat section 2 are unchanged. The minimum snapshot is 444 bytes and the maximum complete allocation is 51,272 bytes. Those bounds are storage/decoder limits, not measured steady traffic.

Checkpoint/journal replay restores the same barrel heading and next exposure tick before applying retained commands. The local recording imports only after reconstructing and comparing its complete canonical final state. The [HMG evidence](redesign-evidence/W05-hmg-sweep.md) records passing runtime, SQLite and local keyboard-inspector validation alongside three failed automatic four-browser attempts. Network acceptance remains open for input-lead and host-clock findings.

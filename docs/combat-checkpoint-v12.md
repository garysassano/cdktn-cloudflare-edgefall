# Diagnostic combat archive format 12

The combat laboratory uses archive **12**, protocol **3.11**, combat snapshot section **3**, and simulation/recording format **9**. Earlier experimental schemas are rejected. This extends [format 11](combat-checkpoint-v11.md) with safe moving entry and a shared body-presence contract. Production v3 integration remains open.

Every controlled player carries `bodyPresence: "present" | "removed"`. Alive requires present; spectating requires removed. Death and protected entry permit either. Non-alive players cannot own a seat or active action. A removed body retains its bounded last position with zero velocity/remainders, no support/contacts, false grounding and airborne locomotion. Snapshots and private checkpoints reject inconsistent combinations. The four-byte word after `lifeStartTick` stores 0 for present and 1 for removed; player records remain 304 bytes, the minimum snapshot 448 bytes and maximum complete allocation 52,060 bytes.

Entry candidates are authored feet anchors against the collision frame's starting geometry. A candidate must have clear standing space and support, then survive the entire frame's platform sweep/carry, remain grounded and end above the fall boundary. Unsafe candidates are skipped in priority order. The accepted body already represents the end of this tick. Checkpoint resets that happen after world movement supply end geometry with zero remaining platform delta.

During the twelve-tick protected entry phase, the same passive physics as death applies gravity, swept collision, support carry and falling after support loss. Input is unavailable and rejected action edges are not deferred. A crush, failed bounded recovery or void removes the entry body and clears protection without spending a life or changing its phase clock. Each subsequent tick retries authored anchors. A successful retry starts a fresh entry animation and protection timer; elapsed time alone cannot activate a removed body.

On the first eligible control tick, life becomes alive before locomotion. The living controller consumes that frame once, allowing a fresh jump to detach from the platform and a fresh fire request to emit one shot. The life owner does not also integrate passive movement on that tick. A solver-confirmed living crush bypasses bullet protection, debits one life and removes the body immediately; unexpected living solver failures still stop the transaction. Ordinary death and respawn delays remain thirty and twelve ticks.

The [entry evidence](redesign-evidence/W03-player-entry.md) covers solid/one-way carry, unsafe anchor fallback, protected crush/void retries, first-control timing, four-player input admission and portable archive/wire continuation, cold SQLite recovery, and native browser rendering/recording replay. These are local engineering proofs. Dedicated airborne/landing art, the complete W06 art/audio benchmark, deployed cadence/traces and production campaign integration remain open.

# Diagnostic combat archive format 19

Archive 19 and combat simulation/recording format 16 add the dedicated tank sacrifice to protocol 3.16. Breakwater mission/recording format 6 and material recording format 3 require the new `specialPressed` command field. Earlier experimental wire/archive/recording formats and content identities reject explicitly. [Archive 18](combat-checkpoint-v18.md) documents the retained cannon continuation contract.

A tank persists its public `special` phase, global action ID, original owner and input generation, arming/commit/end ticks and fixed charge direction. The action allocator, player progress, ownership graph, exact field set and complete snapshot projection are validated at every accepted boundary. The original owner remains valid after ejection, death or connection renewal; future/unknown owners and aliased hardpoint action IDs reject.

`VehicleSpecial` begins only on its own accepted edge while its held bit is set and the seat is occupied. Holding for thirty consecutive accepted ticks commits at `startTick + 29`. Release, control loss, ordinary exit or a damaging hit cancels; held intent alone never restarts a canceled attempt. Damage resolves before commitment, and blocked ejection cancels without consuming armor or removing the driver. Pause/recovery settles an occupied seat and cancels arming. A committed charge is already released and survives those boundaries unchanged until accepted simulation ticks resume.

Safe commitment ejects through the existing full-body candidate/path/support checks, applies normal reboard/protection rules, cancels unreleased hardpoints, advances seat/input generations once and consumes the hull permanently. The unoccupied hull moves on the following tick at six pixels per accepted tick in the committed horizontal direction, using the shared grounded solver. It remains unclaimable and is no longer a damageable controlled vehicle. Previously released bullets and cannon shells remain independent attacks.

A swept hull contact with physical terrain or enemy hurtboxes, a solver wall/ceiling contact, or the sixtieth charge tick produces one eight-damage blast with radius seventy-two pixels and a sixteen-target cap. The blast begins at the hull center and uses impact-time transforms, material occlusion, friendly exclusions and per-entity hit grouping; no direct-hit damage is added. A solver crush or fall settles the wreck without creating an off-stage damage volume. The terminal phase/end tick prevents duplicate blasts after restore. Explosion notices preserve original action/owner identity and the tank source ID; the archive checks their exact terminal boundary.

Local inspector V, first/second benchmark keyboards V/Period and standard gamepad right shoulder map separately from Jump/Fire. Accepted arming progress drives an engineering bar and rising engine sample pitch, followed by ejection and released-charge feedback. Final vehicle media, network audio, production v3 integration, broader impairment and deployed timing remain separate acceptance work.
